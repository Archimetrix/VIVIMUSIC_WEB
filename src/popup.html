Lyrics
Synced Lyrics
Auto-open Lyrics tab

Pulls lyrics data from lrc.red, BiniLyrics, Better Lyrics (incl. Unison, Portato, Legato), Musixmatch, LRCLIB, Spotify — syllable-synced lyrics shown when available, then word-synced, then line-synced, otherwise plain. Use the +/− next to "Lyrics via …" if a specific song's timing is a little off — it's saved per song and provider.Also can be refetched from other providers

Lyrics alignment

Where the lyrics lines sit in the panel.

Highlight animation

Fill sweep: karaoke-style left-to-right fill on the current word. Glow up: the current word lights up with a soft glow instead. Word bounce: each word pops with a little spring as it's sung. Smooth zoom: the current line scales up while the others shrink back. Apple Music: big bold lines with the others softly blurred. Smooth zoom and Apple Music are always left-aligned.

Lyrics font
Dancing in the moonlight

Changes the font of the lyrics lines only — nothing else on YouTube Music. Fonts are bundled with the extension (no internet needed). Lyrics in scripts a font doesn't cover (Hindi, Bengali, Korean, Japanese…) automatically use the default font.

Show lyrics for video songs

Turn off to skip lyrics on real music videos (official videos, live performances, UGC uploads) — plain songs are unaffected either way. When off, the Lyrics tab shows a little dancer instead. Most video songs may get wrong or poorly synced lyrics, so it's better to keep this off.

Show dancer when no lyrics found

When lyrics genuinely can't be found for a track (video or not), show the dancer animation instead of a plain "No lyrics found" message.

Skip non-music sections (SponsorBlock)

Auto-skips talking/intro/outro parts of music videos and keeps lyrics timing in sync with the skip.

Customize provider priority

Off: every enabled provider is requested at once; once the first reply arrives, the other replies are compared for up to 1 second in the background and the best sync level wins (syllable → word → line) before anything is shown. On: your order below decides — if your 1st choice has nothing for this song it falls through to your 2nd, then 3rd, and whatever a priority provider returns is shown, even if it is only line-synced. Pick up to 3 (syllable/word-capable providers). If none of them has the song, the other providers are used with the same 1-second best-level check.

Wait 2s for higher-priority provider

On: if a lower-priority provider replies first, wait up to 2s to see if a higher one beats it before showing lyrics. Off: whichever of your 3 answers first is shown immediately.

Spotify Canvas
Spotify Canvas

Uses your Spotify session to find the matching Canvas and plays it silently over the YT Music artwork.

When both are available, prefer

If a track has both a Spotify Canvas and Apple Music-style artwork: picking Spotify always prefers it. Picking Apple Music only shows it instantly if that track's artwork is already cached — otherwise Spotify Canvas plays immediately while Apple Music's artwork downloads quietly in the background, ready for instant playback the next time you play that song.
