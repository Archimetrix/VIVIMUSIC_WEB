Lyrics
Synced Lyrics
Auto-open Lyrics tab

Pulls from LRCLIB and the BetterLyrics API — word-synced lyrics shown when available, otherwise line-synced, otherwise plain. Use the +/− next to "Lyrics via …" if a specific song's timing is a little off — it's saved per song and provider.

Lyrics alignment

Where the lyrics lines sit in the panel.

Highlight animation

Fill sweep: karaoke-style left-to-right fill on the current word. Glow up: the current word lights up with a soft glow instead. Word bounce: each word pops with a little spring as it's sung.

Show lyrics for video songs

Turn off to skip lyrics on real music videos (official videos, live performances, UGC uploads) — plain songs are unaffected either way. When off, the Lyrics tab shows a little dancer instead.

Show dancer when no lyrics found

When lyrics genuinely can't be found for a track (video or not), show the dancer animation instead of a plain "No lyrics found" message.

Skip non-music sections (SponsorBlock)

Auto-skips talking/intro/outro parts of music videos and keeps lyrics timing in sync with the skip.

Customize provider priority

Off: every enabled provider above is still requested at once, and whichever gives the best result first wins — same as today. On: still requests everyone at once, but organizes the results by your order below — if your 1st choice has nothing for this song, it falls through to your 2nd, then 3rd. Spotify Canvas
Spotify Canvas

Uses your Spotify session to find the matching Canvas and plays it silently over the YT Music artwork.

When both are available, prefer

If a track has both a Spotify Canvas and Apple Music-style artwork: picking Spotify always prefers it. Picking Apple Music only shows it instantly if that track's artwork is already cached — otherwise Spotify Canvas plays immediately while Apple Music's artwork downloads quietly in the background, ready for instant playback the next time you play that song.

Brave keeps logging you out?

BRAVE FIX

Brave's privacy settings mistake Spotify's persistent login cookies for temporary data and wipe them on close. Fix it permanently in 3 steps.

1Disable hidden "Ephemeral" storage
  1. Open a new tab → brave://flags
  2. Search "Storage"
  3. Find "Enable First Party Ephemeral Storage"
  4. Set it to Disabled
  5. Click Relaunch
2Exempt Spotify from data deletion
  1. Open brave://settings/content/siteData
  2. Under "Allowed to save data," click Add
  3. Enter [*.]spotify.com
  4. Remove Spotify from "Delete on close" if listed
3Clear broken cookies & log back in
  1. Open the Spotify Web Player
  2. Click the 🔒 padlock → Site settings → Clear data
  3. Refresh and log in with your password
⚠️ Avoid temporary 6-digit email login codes — use your password so the session persists.
